FMCG and Trade
Vijay
Enterprises
Connecting the world's consumer brands to every market that matters.

Distribution built for scale. Operated with precision.

Vijay Enterprises is a high-volume FMCG trading venture working alongside some of India's and the world's most trusted consumer brands. What began as a conviction that great brands deserve great distribution has grown into a business with over 200 global brand partnerships and a reach that spans markets across continents.

The company operates with a disciplined approach to logistics, inventory, and last-mile delivery, ensuring that every brand partnership is backed by operational excellence at every stage. The focus is not just on volume, but on building relationships that compound over time.

Every distributor relationship is built on transparency and consistency. Every brand partnership is treated as a long-term commitment, not a transactional arrangement. That standard has made Vijay Enterprises a trusted name across a growing global network.

All Ventures
Healthcare
RVR Super
Speciality
Coastal Karnataka's most advanced hospital. World-class medicine, where people actually live.

An institution built on one belief: quality care is not a privilege.

RVR Super Speciality Centre was founded on a straightforward conviction: the quality of medical care a patient receives should not be determined by which city they live in. In Coastal Karnataka, that conviction became an institution.

The centre brings together the largest neurology team in Coastal Karnataka, alongside specialists across Cardiology, Oncology, Urology, ENT, Rheumatology, and Nephrology, under a single roof in Mangaluru. Every department is led by specialists with decades of clinical experience, operating with technology and protocols that match the country's top metropolitan hospitals.

RVR is also home to Mangalore's first dedicated Bladder Clinic, a reflection of the institution's commitment to identifying and addressing the most critical needs in regional healthcare. The hospital operates 12+ hours of daily OPD and 24x7 emergency services because access to care cannot be limited by the clock.

Entertainment
Vijay
Productions
Indian storytelling, produced to the standard the audience deserves.

Cinema as culture. Produced with conviction.

Vijay Productions was founded on the belief that Indian cinema has always had the stories. What it needs is the conviction to tell them at the highest possible level of craft and ambition. That is the mandate this company exists to fulfil.

The company backs mainstream films with genuine cultural weight — stories that reflect the breadth and complexity of India, produced with the resources, talent, and intentionality that audiences across the country deserve. Every project is selected not just for its commercial potential, but for what it contributes to Indian cinema as a whole.

Vijay Productions operates with the long-term in mind. The goal is not to produce a single hit, but to build a body of work that represents a consistent standard of quality in Indian entertainment. Films that travel. Stories that last.

The Group

Built with
conviction.

A collective united not by a single sector, but by a commitment to integrity and quality that does not change with scale.

What this group stands for.

Vijay MN Shetty Group is not defined by any single industry. It is defined by the way it operates across all of them. Every company in this portfolio, regardless of sector, is held to the same principles: build with integrity, deliver quality that earns trust, and think in decades rather than quarters.

The group spans consumer trade, healthcare, entertainment, and more. Each company is independently led and operationally distinct. But the founder's involvement and the standard he holds each venture to is what ties the portfolio together.

Ethical leadership is not an initiative here. It is the operating model. Every decision reflects a commitment to doing things the right way, even when a faster or cheaper path exists. This group will keep growing. The standard will not change with scale.

Vision
A group that sets the standard for ethical, high-quality business across every industry it enters.
Mission
To build companies with integrity and create institutions that serve people well and last.
Standard
The industry changes. The quality of what we build does not.

A note from the Founder

I have always believed that business, done right, is one of the most powerful tools for creating lasting value — for people, for communities, and for the country.

That belief is what drives every decision in this group. Not the size of the market, not the speed of the return, but whether what we are building will genuinely serve the people it is meant to serve, and whether it will do so with the quality and integrity they deserve.

The companies in this group operate across different industries. Consumer trade. Healthcare. Entertainment. And others that will follow. What connects them is not a common theme but a common approach: enter with a long-term commitment, hire people who care about getting it right, and build institutions rather than projects.

Ethical leadership is not something we aspire to. It is the baseline. When you hold that standard consistently, trust follows. And trust, in any business, is the most durable competitive advantage there is.

India is at a point where institutions built with integrity today will compound in value for generations. That is the opportunity this group is committed to. Not a quick one. A lasting one.

Vijay MN ShettyFounder, Vijay MN Shetty Group
